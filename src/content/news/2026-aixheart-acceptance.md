date: 2026-08-09
title: Short paper accepted to AIxHeart 2026, to appear in the IEEE Xplore Digital Library.
---

Our short paper, "Learning to Use AI, Learning to Overlook Its Risks? Canadian Teens' AI Risk Perception and the Social Conditions of Guidance," has been accepted to the AIxHeart 2026 conference and will be formally published in the IEEE Xplore Digital Library. The paper is authored by Chuxuan Hou, Belinda Kwan, Sophia Zhao, Daniel Hou, Sophia Hou, Xuan He Wang, Zixin Yin, Baidu Zhou, and Tao Wang. The conference will be held in Laguna Hills, California, September 28–30, 2026.
