Do AI agents help high school students learn and grow, or do they quietly do the thinking for them? This project studies how AI agents affect the learning and development of high school students. Participants take part in a real research project from the first question to a finished paper: designing the study, collecting and analyzing data, and writing up the results.

### At a glance

- **Duration:** November 2026 to February 2027 (4 months)
- **Commitment:** about 6 hours per week, plus a 2-hour team meeting every two weeks (online or in person)
- **Positions:** 2 students
- **Sponsorship contribution:** CAD $3,000 to the lab, supporting research labor and AI token costs for the project
- **Application deadline:** October 30
- **Selection:** shortlisted applicants are interviewed; positions are offered only after a successful interview

### What you will do

- Read and summarize research on AI and adolescent learning
- Help design the study, such as a survey, interviews, or a small experiment
- Collect and process data, run statistical analyses, and make figures
- Work with the research team to write the final paper

### What you will gain

- Hands-on experience with the full research process, from topic to paper
- Training in research methods, data analysis, and academic writing
- Regular guidance from a research mentor
- A completed research paper written with the team

### Who we are looking for

- Students currently in Grade 10 or 11
- Real curiosity about research, and a willingness to read, ask questions, and revise
- Programming experience (for example, Python) is a plus but not required
- Reliability, good communication, and the ability to meet weekly deadlines

### How to apply

Send a short application to [hello@sialab.org](mailto:hello@sialab.org) by **October 30**. Please include:

- Your name, grade, school, and contact information
- A short statement of about 200 words on why this topic interests you

Questions? Contact us at [hello@sialab.org](mailto:hello@sialab.org).
