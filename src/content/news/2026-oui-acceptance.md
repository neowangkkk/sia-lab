Our submission to the 2026 Open and User Innovation (OUI) Conference at Harvard has been accepted. The paper, "Preserving Disagreement in Human-AI Innovation: An LLM Multi-Agent Replay Study," is authored by Tao Wang (University of Toronto) and Shengsheng Huang (Texas A&M University).
